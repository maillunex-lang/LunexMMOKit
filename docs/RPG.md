# RPG
Source level curve and cap take precedence; determine actual cap from progression logic, not LevelConfig alone. XP from campaign and activities. Equipment slots, rarity, enhancement, dismantle, talents, cosmetic wings, mounts, pets. Battle power derived from stats, not direct damage multiplier. Monetization cosmetics, optional pass and convenience; no paid exclusive combat power, no paid random rewards at launch. Numbers provisional until playtests.
