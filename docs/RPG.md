# RPG
Level cap proposal 60 for initial release; vertical slice 1–10. XP from campaign and activities. Equipment slots, rarity, enhancement, dismantle, talents, cosmetic wings, mounts, pets. Battle power derived from stats, not direct damage multiplier. Offline progression capped at 8 hours initially, server-clock and idempotent claims. No offline PvP. Economy sinks and sources audited. Monetization cosmetics, optional pass and convenience; no paid exclusive combat power, no paid random rewards at launch. Numbers provisional until playtests.
